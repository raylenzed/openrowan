## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I win?

You run the line for ten years (forty quarters). Every spring a council checks that fares and subsidies cover enough of the running costs. Miss the target for two years in a row while local support is low, and the line is closed. Survive the ten years, and the better your finances, support and passenger numbers, the better your ending.

### Can I change the language?

The game follows your iPhone's language (Japanese, otherwise Simplified Chinese). The 日本語 / 中文 button on the map switches it at any time.

### Is the railway real?

No. The line, the towns and the people are fictional. The situations, such as bus replacement, unstaffed stations and separating track from operations, are real problems that regional railways face.

### Which languages does the App support?

Japanese and Simplified Chinese.

### How do I turn off usage statistics or delete what you have about me?

Open Settings (the gear button) in the App. You can switch the usage-statistics toggle off at any time, and the delete button removes the statistics stored about your device from our server.

### How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
