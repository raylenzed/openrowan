## Overview

RigReady ("the App") is developed by Leilei Zhang. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and where it is stored.

**Short version:** RigReady is a local-first RV checklist app. Your checklists, custom items, and run progress stay on your device. We do not operate a server for this App and we do not collect your data.

## Data We Access

### Local Storage

RigReady stores your checklists locally on your device. This can include your rig type, checklist items and sections you create or edit, check-off progress, and seasonal reminder settings.

### Notifications (Optional)

If you enable a seasonal maintenance reminder, the App asks for notification permission and schedules the reminder locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

### Purchases

The one-time "RigReady Pro" unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

## What We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect your name, email, or contact information.
- We do not upload your checklists or usage data to any server.
- We do not track your usage patterns or behavior.
- We do not use advertising networks or analytics SDKs.
- We do not require an account or login.
- We do not access your photos, location, or contacts.

## Data Storage

All RigReady records are stored on your device. Leilei Zhang has no access to the data you enter into the App.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
