## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I set my rig type?

RigReady asks for your rig type on first launch (Class A, Class B, Class C, travel trailer, fifth wheel, pop-up, or truck camper). Your departure checklist is pre-filled to match. You can change the rig type later from the library screen.

### How do I run a checklist?

Open a checklist and tap each row to check it off. Progress saves automatically — if you get interrupted mid-walkaround, your checks are still there when you come back.

### How do I re-use a checklist on the next trip?

Open the checklist menu and choose **Reset**. The finished run is cleared and the list is ready again. Your custom items always stay.

### Can I add my own items?

Yes. Open a checklist, choose **Edit**, and add, reorder, group, or reword any item. Free users can fully customize their rig's departure checklist.

### What does RigReady Pro include?

Pro is a one-time $5.99 purchase that unlocks every rig type, arrival checklists, and the winterize / de-winterize seasonal packs. No subscription.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open any locked checklist and tap **Restore Purchases** on the unlock screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that bought Pro.

### How do I share a checklist?

Open the checklist menu and choose **Share**. The list is sent as plain text through the iOS share sheet (AirDrop, Messages, Mail).

### Does RigReady work offline?

Yes, completely. Network is only used by Apple's App Store system during purchase or restore.
