## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I add a pottery piece?

Tap the **+** button on the Pieces screen. Add a title, stage, forming method, clay body, form, size, weight, glaze notes, and studio notes. Save the piece when you are done.

### Can I attach photos to a piece?

Yes. Open or create a piece, then use the photo attachment control in the editor. ClayTrace only uses the photos you choose, and they stay on your device.

### How do I record a firing?

Open the Firings tab and tap **+**. You can record the firing type, date, kiln, cone, peak temperature, hold, atmosphere, shelf layout, outcome, and notes.

### How do I save glaze notes?

Open the Glazes tab and tap **+**. You can save the glaze name, maker or source, cone, clay body, layering notes, and outcome notes.

### Where is my data stored?

Your records are stored locally on your device. ClayTrace does not require an account and does not upload your studio records to a server.

### How do I back up my records?

Open Settings and use **Export full JSON backup**. This creates a backup file that includes your pieces, firings, glazes, and attached photos. You can save it anywhere the iOS Files app can reach.

### Can I export a spreadsheet?

Yes. Open Settings and use **Export pieces CSV** to create a CSV file for spreadsheet apps.

### Can I export a single piece?

Yes. Open a piece detail screen and use the share button to export a PDF record for that piece.

### How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) to request one.
