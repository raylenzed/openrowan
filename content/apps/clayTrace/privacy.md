## Overview

ClayTrace ("the App") is developed by Leilei Zhang. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and where it is stored.

**Short version:** ClayTrace is a local-first pottery studio log. Your pieces, glaze notes, firing records, exports, and attached photos stay on your device. We do not operate a server for this App and we do not collect your studio records.

## Data We Access

### Local Storage

ClayTrace stores your pottery records locally on your device. This can include piece titles, clay bodies, forms, sizes, weights, stages, glaze notes, firing information, outcomes, ratings, studio notes, and export files you create.

### Photos You Choose

ClayTrace can access photos only when you choose to attach photos to a piece. The selected photos are stored locally as part of your studio record. We do not upload or review these photos.

### Exported Files

The App can export JSON backups, CSV files, and PDF piece records through the iOS share sheet. You decide where those files are saved or shared.

## What We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect your name, email, or contact information.
- We do not upload your pottery records, firing notes, glaze notes, or photos to our servers.
- We do not track your usage patterns or behavior.
- We do not use advertising networks or analytics SDKs.
- We do not require an account or login.
- We do not share your studio records with third parties.

## Data Storage

All ClayTrace records are stored on your device. Leilei Zhang has no access to the data you enter into the App.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.
