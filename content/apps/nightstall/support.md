## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does a night work?

Each night you choose what to stock, set your prices and pick where to put your cart. Then the market opens and the night plays out. Unsold ingredients spoil, rain keeps crowds away, and a rival selling the same food will take customers from you. You get a short summary at the end of every night.

### Is there a time limit or energy system?

No. A night takes three to five minutes, you can stop between nights, and nothing makes you wait or pay to keep playing. There are no ads.

### My game progress is gone.

Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app, or switching to a new iPhone without restoring from a backup, removes it.

### Which languages does the App support?

Traditional Chinese.

### How do I turn off usage statistics or delete what you have about me?

Open Settings (the gear button) in the App. You can switch the usage-statistics toggle off at any time, and the delete button removes the statistics stored about your device from our server.

### How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
