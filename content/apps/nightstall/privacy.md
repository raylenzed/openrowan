## Overview

NightStall (夜市攤王) ("the App") is developed by Leilei Zhang. It is a night-market stall management game set in Taiwan. This page explains what the App does and does not do with your data.

**Short version:** there is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your device. If you allow it, the App counts anonymous usage, for example how far players get, so the game can be improved. You can turn that off at any time.

## What We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts
- We do not use advertising networks or advertising identifiers
- We do not track you across other apps or websites
- We do not require an account or login

## Data Storage

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the App removes them.

## Crash Reports

If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. They contain your device model, iOS version and a technical stack trace, and no personal information.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.
