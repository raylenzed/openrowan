## Overview

Stridebook ("the App") is developed by Leilei Zhang. It is an offline running log. This page explains what the App does — and does not — do with your data.

**Short version:** your runs, shoes, races and notes are stored only on your device, and nothing from Apple Health ever leaves it. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. From version 1.1, if you allow it, the App counts anonymous usage (which features are used, never what you logged) so it can be improved. You can turn that off in Settings.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, Stridebook **reads** your running and walking workouts from Apple Health so they can be added to your log. This access is optional, read-only and can be turned off at any time in the Health app (Profile → Apps → Stridebook).

- Stridebook never writes to Apple Health.
- Health data is used only on your device to fill in your log. It is never sent anywhere, never included in usage statistics, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or marketing.

## What We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect your name, location or contacts
- We do not use advertising networks or advertising identifiers
- We do not track you across other apps or websites
- We do not use third-party analytics services
- We do not send any run, shoe, race or health data off your device
- We do not require an account or login

## Data Storage

Everything you enter or import is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your log is never synced to any server. Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer backup), so it comes back if you restore your device.

CSV exports and backup files are created only when you ask for them, and you choose where to save or share them.

## Purchases

The one-time "Stridebook Pro" purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

## Crash Reports

If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. They contain your device model, iOS version and a technical stack trace, and no personal information.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.
