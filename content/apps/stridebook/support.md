## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I import my runs from Apple Health?

In Stridebook, open Settings → Apple Health, turn on Import runs from Health and allow access to workouts. Stridebook reads your running and walking workouts from the last 90 days — including ones recorded before you installed the app — and keeps importing new ones. Tap Import now to fetch the latest. Workouts that are already in your log are never duplicated.

### Can I bring my history over from Strava or another app?

Yes. Stridebook can import a CSV file, including the `activities.csv` file from a Strava data export. In Strava, request your archive (Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account), unzip it, and open `activities.csv` from Settings → Import old log (CSV) in Stridebook.

### What is free and what does Stridebook Pro add?

Logging runs, Apple Health import and the last four weeks of history are free. Stridebook Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks your full history and charts, unlimited shoes and races, and CSV/backup export. There is no subscription.

### How does shoe tracking work?

Add your shoes and pick one when you log a run. Stridebook adds up the distance on each pair and shows how close it is to the retirement distance you set, so you know when it's time for a new pair.

### Can I change units or the first day of the week?

Yes. In Settings you can switch between kilometres and miles and choose which day your training week starts on.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

In Settings, tap Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Stridebook Pro.

### How do I back up or move my data?

With Pro, use Settings → Back up everything to save a full backup file, then Restore from backup on your new device. Your data is also included in your normal iPhone backup.

### How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
