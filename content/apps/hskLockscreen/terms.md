### Free and Pro access

HSK 1–2 are free. An optional one-time, non-consumable Pro purchase unlocks HSK 3–6 meanings, pronunciation, favorites and widget use. Higher-level hanzi and pinyin can be previewed for free. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.

The App Store purchase sheet displays the applicable local price before confirmation. Apple processes payment. Use **Widget → Unlock HSK 3–6 → Restore purchases** with the Apple Account used for the original purchase to restore eligible access. Refunded or revoked purchases may no longer grant Pro access. Refund requests can be submitted to Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Learning content and device behavior

The word bank follows **HSK 2.0**, levels 1–6. Meanings are brief study aids and may not cover every use of a word. Pronunciation is generated by the system Mandarin voice. The app is an independent learning aid, is not affiliated with Chinese Testing International, and does not promise examination results.

You add widgets through your device's widget controls. Rotation preferences select a schedule; iOS controls when widgets actually refresh. We do not guarantee an exact refresh time. Offline study does not require an account; purchases, restores, web support pages and initial system-voice downloads require the relevant network services.
