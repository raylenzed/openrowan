## DriftLog Support

DriftLog measures how accurately your watches really run — in seconds per day — by comparing your taps against network-synced reference time. No microphone, no quiet room, works on mechanical and quartz.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I measure a watch?

Open the watch, tap **Measure**, and press the button at the exact moment the second hand crosses the 12. Repeat a day or more later; DriftLog computes seconds per day from the interval between readings.

### Why do I need at least two readings?

Rate is drift over time. One reading sets the baseline; the next reading tells DriftLog how far the watch wandered and over how long.

### Can it measure quartz watches?

Yes — that is DriftLog's specialty. Quartz drift shows up over weeks, so measure once now and again in a few weeks; the App reports seconds per month for quartz movements.

### Does it work offline?

All your data lives on the device and every screen works offline. Only two things use the network: fetching reference time for a new measurement, and App Store purchases.

### What does DriftLog Pro unlock?

Unlimited watches, the full service & regulation log, and CSV export — as a one-time purchase. Your first watch is free with every measurement feature included.

### How do I restore my purchase?

Settings → **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

### How do I export my data?

Settings → **Export CSV** (Pro). Your measurements and service records export as a standard CSV file you can open in any spreadsheet.
